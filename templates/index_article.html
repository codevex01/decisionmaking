Article 16: "Weights by ENvelope and SLOpe" combined with "Alternative Ranking Technique based on Adaptive Standardized Intervals"

🔬 Scientific Methodology

WENSLO: Calculates objective criteria weights using envelope-slope mathematics

ARTASI: Ranks alternatives using adaptive standardized intervals with ideal/anti-ideal usefulness

Advantage: Eliminates subjective bias in weight determination while providing robust ranking
